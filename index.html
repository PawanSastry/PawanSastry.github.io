AI-powered Nurturing Platform

Built a CRM nurturing capability using Campaign, Message and Decision AI, with human oversight, to support renewals, move-ins and win-back journeys across email and SMS.

~$15MARR contribution to the Knock AI SKU
  1. Challenge

    Improve customer engagement and retention through more effective, scalable nurturing journeys.

  2. Approach

    Bring campaign creation, messaging and decision support together, while retaining human oversight in the workflow.

  3. Outcome

    Campaign creation increased 40%; approximately $3.4M of a $5M at-risk book was retained.

Knock Data Cloud

Launched a self-service reporting product on a re-architected Snowflake and dbt foundation, serving customers across 785 sites.

−70%Query latency · 46% lower cloud cost
  1. Challenge

    Improve reporting responsiveness and create a scalable self-service data experience.

  2. Approach

    Re-architect the data models and reporting foundation using Snowflake and dbt. The governed model now powers Knock Data Cloud and the analytics portfolio.

  3. Outcome

    70% lower query latency, 46% lower cloud cost and $93K ARR across 785 sites.

Unified Identity & Authentication

17 → 3Monthly user-management tickets
  1. Challenge

    Improve reliability of user access and reduce operational support burden.

  2. Approach

    Focused on the underlying synchronisation and race-condition problems affecting identity flows, and standardised user-assignment flows.

  3. Outcome

    Monthly tickets reduced by 82%, with the improvement sustained for more than six months.

Unified Renter & Marketing Analytics
